ETHOS Event Collective

About ETHOS

As an Experience and Design Intern at ETHOS Event Collective, a destination management company with locations across the U.S., I worked on the planning stages of client programs at the Orlando office. In this role, I helped initiate proposal development by managing internal RFPs, contacting vendors to gather accurate and competitive quotes, and inputting vendor details into proposals and internal costing software. I also supported the creative development of event designs and participated in on-site visits to assess venues, ensuring proposal accuracy and alignment with client expectations. This position required me to quickly adapt to fast turnarounds and evolving client needs while contributing to the overall success of the Experience and Design team.

Projects

  • Proposal Development: Supported around fifteen client programs over the summer, shelling RFPs into proposal templates, creating initial design concepts based on client aesthetics, and sourcing vendor pricing and availability for event looks, menus, activations, entertainment, and gifting.
  • Vendor & Costing Coordination: Gathered and entered vendor details into both proposal documents and the internal CRM/costing system, building daily professional communication skills through ongoing vendor correspondence.
  • On-Site Venue Visits: Participated in venue site visits to verify proposal accuracy and ensure designs aligned with client expectations.
