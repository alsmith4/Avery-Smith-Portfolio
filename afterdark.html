AfterDark

About AfterDark

AfterDark is a late-night event held six times per semester for ISU students as an alternative to substance use. Our executive planning team is made up of seven members and our advisor. We plan and execute activities such as bingo, karaoke, a novelty act, and we even bring in entertainment like Jerry Trainor from iCarly.

I have been a member for two years and held two different positions. My first year, I served as the Vice President. In this role, I coordinated a schedule for all six of our events throughout the year to ensure each activity was staffed. I worked to recruit and maintain volunteers, as well as led the meeting before each event to promote cohesive relationships between all members.

As President, I oversee all operations of the organization and serve as the primary point of contact. I prepare and submit event authorizations to ensure clear communication with event space managers and maintain consistent collaboration with the organization's advisor to address and resolve issues. I lead weekly executive meetings to align the board on goals, facilitate smooth operations, and drive the organization's overall success.

Projects

  • Event Authorizations: Complete and submit university event authorizations two weeks ahead of each event, coordinating endorsements from the Treasurer and Advisor before university approval.
  • Team Scheduling & Onboarding: Coordinate semester kickoff logistics for the exec board, including office hours, Clubfest signups, meeting availability, and planning the team retreat.
  • Constitution & Operations Manual Update: Revised the club's Constitution and Operations Manual to align with updated university policies, coordinating with the Advisor before submission.
  • Event Hosting & Announcements: Speak to crowds before entertainers take the stage to promote activities, upcoming events, and social media.
  • Volunteer Scheduling: Recruited and scheduled volunteers for each event based on availability and preferences, staying in close contact to ensure smooth staffing.
  • Event Night Meetings: Created agendas and icebreakers, including a scavenger hunt, to lead pre-event meetings for execs, supervisors, and volunteers.
  • Volunteer Appreciation Social: Planned and coordinated a social event for volunteers and execs to build community and reinforce the value of volunteer contributions.
