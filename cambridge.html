Cambridge Investment Research

About Cambridge

Cambridge Investment Research, Inc. is a broker-dealer located in Fairfield, Iowa. They serve over 3,800 financial professionals in multiple facets. I was a member of the Marketing and Communications team as the Advertising and Media Relations Intern. My job involved all things advertising and sponsorships at the national and local levels. Upon receiving an opportunity, I researched and communicated with external companies to write a well-rounded proposal that would aid my supervisors in their decision of whether or not to move forward. After committing to a sponsorship or advertising opportunity, I would work to coordinate the deliverables for each project. Sponsorships often included event presence, so I had the opportunity to organize events. In our role, we juggled multiple projects at a time with varying deadlines and requirements. It was vital to have excellent organizational and time management skills and to be able to adapt to shifting priorities. I often coordinated with other teams to complete projects on time. I had to adapt to our project management system to send and receive projects and set proper deadlines for my coworkers to ensure the timely completion of projects.

Projects

  • National & Local Airport Advertising: Researched and proposed airport advertising for a national event (Denver) and a year-long campaign (Cedar Rapids, Des Moines, Phoenix, Mesa). Built out full PowerPoint proposals with location comparisons, maps, and recommendations for leadership.
  • Local Newspaper Advertising: Evaluated ad size/pricing options for a local paper's annual business feature and independently recommended a 1/4 page placement, then managed the ad redesign and invoicing process.
  • Golden Pitch Sponsorship Proposal: Researched and pitched a sponsorship for Northern Arizona University's business school pitch contest, compiling reach data and details into a polished proposal.
  • Pride on the Riverfront Event Sponsorship: Coordinated Cambridge's on-site presence at the 2024 event, including branded giveaways, booth signage, and event logistics from concept through execution.
  • Citywire Ad Copy Creation: Wrote multiple 75-character ad copy variations for national publication placements, several of which were selected for use.
