ISU Research Park

About ISU Research Park

At the Iowa State University Research Park, I work as part of a team of 3–5 marketing interns responsible for managing the Park's social media, newsletter, job board, podcast, and events. While we collaborate across all areas, my main focus is running the social media and podcast. I manage and schedule content for four platforms, posting at least three times per week to promote events and highlight activity around the Park, while tracking analytics to identify trends and refine strategies. I also help lead the podcast, developing episode concepts, coordinating guests, planning interview outlines, and handling recording and editing to ensure a polished final product. We often assist with event set-up, photography, and tear-down. This role has strengthened my creativity, organization, and communication skills while teaching me how to manage multiple projects and adapt to new priorities.

Projects

  • Social Media Management: Manage and schedule content across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X (plus the Economic Development and Industry Relations Facebook page) using Meta Business Suite, restructuring an inconsistent calendar into a consistent 3x/week posting schedule and tracking analytics to refine strategy.
  • Social Media Tracking System: Consolidated scattered documents into a single master spreadsheet with brand pillars to keep content aligned, plus a dedicated content planning page for reels covering concept, filming details, and participants.
  • The Innovators Podcast: Help produce a podcast series on entrepreneurs and innovators, researching and booking guests, conducting interviews for the Young Innovators segment, and editing/publishing final episodes — including a first solo interview and a co-hosted episode with our Associate Director.
